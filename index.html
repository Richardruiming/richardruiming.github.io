$ cat why_love

I major in what I love. I find myself one of the few that would say: "I hate spending 8 hours stepping through RISC-V codes, but from deep down my heart, I am amazed by the intricacy of memory mapping and privilege control of an OS.

$ cat why_discipline

I am a disciplined person, not just academically. I consider this the #1 characteristic that makes me distinct. To me, success is waking up at 6:30am when 10am is considered early in college; success is spending my Friday nights at the gym when drinking is the norm.

$ cat why_learning

I want to continue to learn. I realized that undergraduate is a starting point rather than a destination. RTL design is more than connecting IC chips on a large scale then calling myself an ASIC engineer, it is the beginning of understanding synthesis tools and timing analysis. I want to stay in touch with the front of this fast changing world.

$ Biggest Challenge?

People are always in disbelief after knowing that I didn't grow up speaking English and I achieved a native-level proficiency in just 5 years.

Unlike many others, I didn't have the environment. I learned this language the hard way by flipping through vocabulary cards. I remember raising my hand for every single question during math class in high school, just to stand up and stutter for 10 seconds before the teacher asked me to go back to my seat because I couldn't articulate the idea even though I knew the answer every time.
